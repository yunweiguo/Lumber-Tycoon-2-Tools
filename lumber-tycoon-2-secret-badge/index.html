Lumber Tycoon 2 Secret Badge: post-nerf checklist

This is a time-sensitive event route. The quest changed during the event, so old videos may show extra entrance steps that no longer apply.

Before you start

Bring a Work Light, a vehicle if possible, and an axe. Complete enough of the Hunt wood task to obtain the Purple Bag / globe-related quest items.

1. Get permission

Use the newer truss cave entrance, reach the Skeleton NPC and choose the permission dialogue. The current route no longer needs the original entrance setup described by early guides.

2. Find the correct glorb

Open the quest globe after permission. Community reports disagree on a fixed click count, while current guides describe a target color sequence ending in orange. Watch the color state instead of trusting a blind count.

3. Deliver it through the cave

Carry the final orange-state globe to the lava section and drop it into the correct opening. Continue to the purple room and collect the three required objects.

4. Finish the bunker puzzle

Deliver the three objects to the bunker, open the boxes, then match each object to the corresponding character/plate. The reward sequence produces the Purple Axe and secret badge.

If it does not trigger

First confirm Skeleton permission, then verify you did not cycle the glorb past the final orange state. Current players also report failed door triggers even after following the route, so server rejoin/retry may be necessary.
