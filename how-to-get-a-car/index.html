How to get your first car in Lumber Tycoon 2

For current Hunt newcomers, “how do I get a car?” is one of the clearest onboarding searches. The starter Utility Vehicle is cheap, but you need land before placing it.

1. Own a plot of land

You need a land plot before the boxed vehicle can be placed and spawned.

2. Save $400

The starter Utility Vehicle costs $400 at Wood R Us. It is a fixed shop price, not a player-market estimate.

3. Buy and place the box

Take the vehicle box to the Wood R Us cashier, buy it, then place the spawner on your land.

4. Respawn cost

The standard Utility Vehicle respawn costs $8. Its small bed makes it a good starter / risky-route vehicle rather than a high-capacity hauler.
