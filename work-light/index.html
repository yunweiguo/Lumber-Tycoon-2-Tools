Where to get a Work Light in Lumber Tycoon 2

The Work Light is a basic but high-intent new-player item because current cave quests make darkness a real friction point.

Price and store

The Work Light is sold at Wood R Us for $80 in-game money.

Why players search it now

The current Hunt secret-badge route sends new players into a dark cave, so guides routinely recommend buying a Work Light before leaving the main shop area.

What to buy next

If you are new, the other major early purchase is the Utility Vehicle so you can move quest objects and wood faster.
