Data policy

Never hide stale market data

Store prices and wood sell values are deterministic game data. Player-to-player trade prices are not. This MVP deliberately marks its trade snapshot as historical instead of pretending it is a live 2026 price feed.

LT2 Tools is an independent fan-made utility site. Not affiliated with Roblox or Defaultio.
Prices labeled historical are community snapshots, not live market quotes.
