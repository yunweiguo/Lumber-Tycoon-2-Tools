Where is the Lost Cave in Lumber Tycoon 2?

The Lost Cave is associated with the snowy / Taiga side of the map. Use the route map for orientation, and treat it as a schematic until exact coordinates are verified.

Fast orientation

From the main spawn / shop area, head toward the snow / Taiga region. The long-running community wiki places the Lost Cave below the Snow Peninsula area and left of the Taiga entrance.

Bring light

The cave is dark enough that a Work Light is a practical requirement for new players. A vehicle reduces the penalty of repeating the route.
