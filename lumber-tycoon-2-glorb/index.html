How the LT2 glorb step works

The glorb step is where many current Hunt players get stuck. Use state verification rather than a hard-coded click count.

Permission comes first

Talk to the Skeleton NPC and obtain permission before opening / cycling the quest globe. Skipping this prerequisite can prevent later steps from registering.

Do not trust one magic number

Current written guides and player reports disagree on the exact number of interactions. One updated guide says 18, while community reports vary. The safer rule is: follow the required color sequence and stop on the final orange state.

After orange

Carry the globe through the cave and place it into the lava-side opening used by the quest. If the next door does not react, re-check permission and glorb state before repeating the entire route.

Why this page exists

Search intent here is troubleshooting, not lore. A production version should collect current failure reports, patch date, server behavior and screenshots for each state.
